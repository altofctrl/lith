02 | out of the boxit's a pomodoro timer.

Right now your lith runs its founding firmware: a focus timer built on the pomodoro method. Twenty-five minutes of work, five of rest, repeat. All on your desk.

  • Turn the wheel to set the length, then tap the first key to start. Tap it again to pause, hold it to reset the phase.
    [ image placeholder: step 1 ]
  • The screen counts down. That's it. That's the interface.
    [ image placeholder: step 2 ]
  • When a block ends, lith buzzes and rolls into a 5 minute break. Tap the second key to skip ahead; hold it to choose which setting the wheel edits.
    [ image placeholder: step 3 ]
  • Every fourth break is a long one.
    [ image placeholder: step 4 ]
by the way: the QR splash you scanned retires itself after the first ten boots. after that your lith just gets on with it.

03 | one stone, many toolsthe timer is just the first shape.

lith is a reconfigurable computer. The pomodoro firmware is one arrangement of it, the way a hand axe is one arrangement of a flint. You can reshape yours: a macropad, a bus countdown, a word of the day display or knap it right back to the default pomodoro whenever you want.

knap /nap/ · verb · to shape a stone tool by striking flakes from it. Here: to shape your lith into a new tool by describing the tool you want.

04 | knapp your owntwo ways to shape it.

However you'd rather work: talk to an agent, or open an IDE and wire it up yourself. Both start from the same hardware.

agent

chat with oldowan

Oldowan is the agent you knapp with, named after the oldest known stone toolkit. Tell it what you want your lith to become, answer a few multiple-choice questions, and it writes the firmware and walks you through flashing it. No code required, though you can read every line it writes.

you'll need a laptop for the flashing part. Knapping flashes new firmware onto your lith over USB, straight from the browser using Web Serial. That works in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. It does not work from phones, and not in Firefox or Safari, which don't support Web Serial. You can start chatting and shaping from the phone you're holding, then open the same link on a laptop to flash; your conversation travels with it.

05 | the knapperytools are for sharing.

Every knapp can be published to the community, where anybody can flash it, star it, and discuss it under the listing, like a forum stitched to a toolbank. Always read the source before you flash something a stranger knapped. A report button is on every listing.
