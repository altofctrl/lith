What this does not show

  • No human comparison group, by design. The instrument's alignment with human judgement is borrowed from Luera et al., who validated it on full-size professionally designed web screens. Nothing here establishes that these judges track human perception of these screens.
  • Two reps per model, eleven model-tiers, fifteen judged builds. Enough to see a tier-scale difference; not enough to rank adjacent models.
  • Six still frames per build. lith's display is animated, and the fluid fill that carries state in the better builds is exactly what a still throws away.
  • One brief. Every build answers the same meeting cost meter prompt.
  • The judges' own low agreement, above, limits every absolute number on this page.
